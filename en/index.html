Discover

A place like no other

Nestled between Avignon and L'Isle-sur-la-Sorgue, at the gateway to the Luberon, La Garance en Provence is a character-filled estate combining authentic charm with contemporary comfort. This former stagecoach stop has been lovingly restored, preserving exposed stone walls, antique beams, and stone floors.

The property offers stunning views of Château de Thouzon and is set within grounds shaded by century-old plane trees. Available for exclusive rental, it welcomes up to 12 guests in 6 refined bedrooms, each with its own private shower room.

Régis and Chantal warmly welcome you to their family home.

Frequently asked questions

How many guests can La Garance accommodate?

La Garance sleeps up to 12 guests in 6 bedrooms, each with a private Italian-style shower room. The property is rented exclusively to a single group.

Where is La Garance en Provence?

In Le Thor (84250), Vaucluse, between Avignon and L'Isle-sur-la-Sorgue, at the gateway to the Luberon. It is a restored 17th-century former coaching inn, classified as a 3-star tourist furnished rental.

How are the six bedrooms arranged?

Les Roses: first floor, double bed (2 × 90 cm) or 2 separate beds, view over the plane-tree courtyard. Les Ocres: first floor, 160 cm bed. Le Pigeonnier: first floor, 160 cm bed, view over the garden and courtyard. La Fenière: first floor, 160 cm bed. La Calade: garden level, 160 cm bed. Les Pierres: garden level with independent access, 160 cm bed.

What are the rates?

Weekly rental of the entire property: €8,000 in high season (July – August), €7,500 in mid season (June and September), €6,800 the rest of the year (October to December). Cleaning fee: €300 per week. Tourist tax not included, payable with the balance.

When is the property available?

The property is not rented from January to May inclusive, nor from 25 September to 31 December 2027. The calendar on the booking page shows the available dates.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in from 4 pm, check-out before 10 am.

What amenities are offered?

17-metre infinity pool, free WiFi, private parking with electric vehicle charging point, covered terrace, pétanque court, table tennis, plancha, bike storage, baby cot and bathtub on request, wellness treatments and massages by appointment.

How do I book?

Choose your dates on the booking page calendar and send your request. Régis will contact you within 24 hours to confirm availability and payment details. A request is not a confirmed booking.
